# Citation & reaction log

A running list of external articles worth referencing — so good sources don't get
lost. Not part of the built site (this file lives outside `content/`).

## How to use it

When you find a good article, add a row. Tag what to do with it:

- **react** — worth a Substack reaction post (bring their tension, answer with our data)
- **cite** — has a hard stat/quote that supports a factor score; cite it in an essay or the relevant profile
- **watch** — interesting but not yet actionable; keep an eye on the story

### Rules for referencing (keep us credible + SEO-safe)
1. Always attribute — outlet, author, date — and link the source.
2. Quote sparingly (a sentence or two). Never reproduce.
3. Always add our own data. The article is the setup; our index is the payoff.
   Pure aggregation is thin content and adds nothing.
4. Prefer the primary source. If a news piece cites a study, cite the study too.
