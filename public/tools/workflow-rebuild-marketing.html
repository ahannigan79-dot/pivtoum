First — the workflow today

How it's run now, by a human

Six steps, start to finish. This is the job exactly as it exists today. Read it whole — this is the world we're about to rebuild.

  1. 1

    Brief & research

    Read the goal, dig through audience data, size up competitors — a day of desk research.

  2. 2

    Creative

    Write 5–10 ad variants, brief a designer, wait, iterate over days.

  3. 3

    Build & target

    Set targeting, budgets and placements by hand in Ads Manager.

  4. 4

    Launch & monitor

    Watch the dashboards daily, pause the losers, react manually.

  5. 5

    Optimise

    Shift budget and tweak creative on a weekly cadence.

  6. 6

    Report

    Pull the numbers, build the deck, format the slides, present.

Then — rebuilt AI-native

The same job, rebuilt

Same six steps, same outcome — now with the machine carrying the load. AI does the volume; you move up to the judgment. The chip on each step shows where your value now lives.

  1. 1

    Brief & research You: the angle

    AI synthesises the research in minutes; you set the strategic angle and the "why now."

  2. 2

    Creative You: brand taste

    AI generates 50 on-brief variants instantly; you curate for brand truth and kill the off-brand.

  3. 3

    Build & target You: the guardrails

    AI configures it from the brief; you approve the spend logic and set the guardrails.

  4. 4

    Launch & monitor You: the exceptions

    AI monitors in real time and reallocates budget; you handle the exceptions and escalations.

  5. 5

    Optimise You: the bets

    AI runs continuous multivariate testing; you own the creative bets and which direction to push.

  6. 6

    Report You: the story

    AI writes the narrative from the data; you own the story to the client and the next-quarter call.

The human change

The impact runs up the ladder

This is the part the headlines miss: AI doesn't hit a career evenly. It eats the execution rungs first — junior and mid — and rewards the two ends: the newcomer who arrives AI-native, and the senior who owns the judgment. Where you stand decides your risk and your move.

Studying / very early career◆ Enter AI-native

You're not on the ladder yet — and the entry rungs you'd have started on are the first to vanish. But you hold the one advantage no one above you has: no habits to unlearn. A clean slate is the perfect place to be AI-native from day one.

Your moveDouble down on the AI-native side of your profile — make it the thing that sets you apart. Nail the understanding of how these workflows really run, and stay razor-aware of how your developing skills map to what the new AI-native work actually needs.
Junior▾ Highest exposure

Your job was the production — building the ads, setting up targeting, pulling the reports. That's exactly what automates first. The rung you'd normally climb is the rung that's disappearing.

Your moveDon't wait your turn up a shortening ladder. Walk in AI-native — direct the tools from day one and skip straight to the judgment work.
Mid-level▾ Squeezed

Execution was half your value — and now it collapses to minutes. You're caught between automated production below and senior judgment above, with no comfortable middle to sit in.

Your moveMove up, fast. Own the strategy and the testing bets, not the button-pushing — become the one who directs the machine, not the one it replaces.
Senior▴ Biggest opportunity

Least exposed — and most amplified. Your value is taste, the strategic call, and the client relationship: precisely what doesn't automate. And now one of you plus AI does the work of a whole team.

⚠ But two catches at the top

  • Someone below you will go AI-native first — if you don't. Seniority is no shield: whoever leads the change controls the path forward, and that can be a junior leapfrogging straight past you.
  • The judgment is now yours alone. With AI on execution and no junior between you and the outcome, there's no one left to blame. You built the AI to your spec; you made the call. AI isn't at fault — you are. Far higher stakes.
Your moveBecome the AI-native operator — before someone below you does. Own the judgment and the relationships, get comfortable carrying the accountability, and lead how your team adopts.
