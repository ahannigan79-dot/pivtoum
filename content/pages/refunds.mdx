# Refunds &amp; cancellation

*Last updated: 7 September 2026*

## The short version

Membership is a subscription you can **cancel any time** from your membership page — the moment you cancel, you're not charged again. If something's genuinely wrong, email **adam@pivotum.ai** and we'll sort it out. We'd rather do right by you than hold onto a payment you're unhappy about.

---

## Cancelling

Open **Manage membership & billing** on your [membership page](/hub/membership) — it takes you to the secure Stripe portal, where you can cancel in a couple of clicks. When you cancel:

- You keep access until the **end of the period you've already paid for**.
- Your subscription then **doesn't renew**, and you're not charged again.

There's no cancellation fee and no need to explain why.

---

## The free trial

If your membership started with a free trial, **cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged at all.** You can do that from the same portal. If you don't cancel, the trial converts to a paid subscription at the price shown at checkout — that's how the trial works, and we tell you so before you start it.

---

## Refunds on a paid period

Membership is billed in advance for each period (for example, each month).

- **Current period.** Because you have full access to the community for the period you've paid for, a paid period is generally **non-refundable** — instead, cancel to stop the next charge. This is the default for most subscriptions.
- **Charged by mistake, or barely used it.** If you were billed right after a renewal you meant to cancel, or you signed up and realised straight away it isn't for you, email us within **14 days** of the charge and we'll refund that period. We're reasonable about this.
- **We ended your membership.** If we end a paid membership for a reason that isn't your conduct or payment, we refund the unused part of the current period.

Refunds go back to your original payment method. We process them within three working days; your bank may take a further five to ten days to show it.

---

## If something went wrong

- **You were double-charged, or charged after cancelling.** Email us — we'll refund it, no quibble.
- **A feature you joined for isn't working.** Tell us. We'd rather fix it (or refund you) than have you stuck.
- **Payment failed / "past due".** Open the billing portal to update your card and keep your access; nothing is lost if you sort it within the grace period.

---

## Your statutory rights

Nothing here removes any refund or cancellation right you have under the consumer law of your country or state — including any statutory cooling-off right. Where such a right applies, it stands alongside this policy.
