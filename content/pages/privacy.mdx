# Privacy

*Last updated: 7 September 2026*

We collect what we need to run Pivotum and the community, and this page says plainly what that is and who else touches it.

Pivotum is operated by **Pivotum LLC**, a limited liability company registered in the State of Delaware, USA. For any question about your data, email **adam@pivotum.ai**.

---

## What we collect

**If you subscribe to the newsletter or get an AI Exposure Report:** your email address, and the role/answers you gave to generate the report. We use these to send you the report and the newsletter.

**If you join Winning in the Age of AI:** you create an account through **Clerk**, our authentication provider, which stores your name, email address, and login credentials. Inside the community we store the things you create and do — your Career Map answers and scores, pod membership and check-ins, posts, comments, messages, reactions, submitted articles/sessions, the workflow documents you generate, your progress, and basic usage (like when you last visited). Your subscription and payment are handled by **Stripe**; we can see that a payment or subscription succeeded and its status, **not your card number**.

**When you use the AI tools:** the text you enter (for example, the workflow you describe for a Rebuild, or your Map answers) is sent to **Anthropic** (the Claude API) to generate your result. Don't paste confidential or personal information about other people into these tools.

**If you turn on phone notifications:** we store a push subscription for your device so we can send you notifications; you can turn this off any time in settings.

**If you just read the site:** anonymous analytics about which pages are visited. Only if you consent, we also use behavioral analytics (Microsoft Clarity) and advertising identifiers (the Meta pixel and Google Ads). See "Analytics, advertising and cookies" below.

## Why we collect it

- **Account details (name, email, login)** — to create and secure your membership
- **Your Map, pods, posts, progress and submissions** — to provide the community and show you your own history
- **What you type into the AI tools** — to generate your report, Map reading, and Workflow Rebuilds
- **Subscription status** — to give you access while you're a paying member, and to handle billing
- **Push subscription** — to send the notifications you opt into
- **Analytics / advertising identifiers** — to understand what's useful and whether our advertising works, if you've consented

We don't sell your data, and we share it only with the providers below, who process it on our behalf.

## Analytics, advertising and cookies

Two things run only with your consent: the **Meta pixel** and **Google Ads** tags (to measure whether our advertising works) and **Microsoft Clarity** (anonymized heatmaps and session replays, to see where the site confuses people).

**If you're in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask first.** On your first visit none of them load unless you accept. Decline and no advertising or behavioral data is collected. Clearing your cookies prompts us to ask again.

**Everywhere else, they load by default — and you can opt out.** We honor your browser's Global Privacy Control ("Do Not Sell or Share My Data") setting.

Either way, declining changes nothing about how the site works. Aside from these, we use no other advertising cookies; anything remaining is strictly necessary — remembering your consent/region and keeping your session working. Clarity's session replays are anonymized and don't capture what you type.

## How long we keep things

- **Newsletter subscribers:** until you unsubscribe (one-click link in every email).
- **Member accounts and content:** while you're a member, and for a period after your subscription ends in case you rejoin — then deleted on request.
- **Billing records:** as long as tax and accounting rules require (typically seven years).
- **Analytics:** aggregated and anonymous, kept for two years.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to **tell you what we hold**, **correct** it, **delete** it, **send you a copy** in a portable format, or **stop** using it for a particular purpose. Email **adam@pivotum.ai** and we'll respond within 30 days. Deleting your account is also available from your settings.

If you're in the UK or EU you have these rights under GDPR and can complain to your data protection authority. If you're in California you have equivalent rights under the CCPA. **We don't sell personal information under any definition, in any jurisdiction.**
