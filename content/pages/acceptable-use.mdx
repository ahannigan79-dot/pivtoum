# Acceptable Use Policy

*Last updated: 7 September 2026*

Winning in the Age of AI is a small, working community built on trust. These are the rules that keep it that way. They apply to everything you post or do inside the community — the feed, pods, comments, messages, submitted articles and sessions, and shared workflow documents. Breaking them can cost you your place.

---

## The standard we hold

- **Be straight with people.** Real name or a consistent handle, honest about who you are and what you've done. No impersonation.
- **Help more than you take.** Pods and threads work because people show up for each other. Bring something.
- **What's shared in your pod stays in your pod.** Members open up here. Don't screenshot, forward, or repeat someone's private discussion outside the group.
- **Disagree well.** Argue the idea, not the person. Heat is fine; contempt isn't.

---

## Not allowed

- Harassment, bullying, hate speech, or targeting someone because of who they are.
- Sexual content, graphic violence, or anything unlawful.
- Spam, unsolicited promotion, recruiting, or pitching the community for your own product or list.
- Posting other people's private or confidential information — including **your employer's or a client's confidential material**. Don't paste it into the tools either.
- Content you don't have the right to share, or that infringes someone's copyright, trademark, or contract.
- Scraping the community, sharing your login, reselling access, or trying to reach areas you haven't paid for.
- Using the AI tools to produce material intended to deceive, defraud, or harm someone.

---

## AI output is your responsibility

The Judgment Gym, Workflow Rebuilds, and Map readings are AI-assisted. Treat every output as a **draft to verify**, not an answer to trust — especially before you use it in your real work, hand it to an employer or client, or act on it. You're responsible for what you do with it. (See the [Terms](/terms) for more.)

---

## Reporting

See something that breaks these rules? Use the **Report** action on a post, or email **adam@pivotum.ai**. Reports come to the founder and are handled discreetly.

---

## What happens if you break them

Depending on severity, we may remove content, give a warning, remove you from a pod, or suspend or end your membership. For serious cases — harassment, unlawful content, or anything that puts other members at risk — we act immediately. If we end a paid membership for a breach, we refund the unused part of your current period.

We apply this judgment in good faith to protect the community. Questions: **adam@pivotum.ai**.
